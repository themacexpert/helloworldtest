# Lorem Ipsum

Lorem ipsum is placeholder text used to fill a layout before the real copy is
ready. It lets designers and developers judge typography, spacing, and line
length without being distracted by the meaning of the words.

## Where it comes from

The text is a scrambled excerpt from *de Finibus Bonorum et Malorum*, a treatise
on ethics written by Cicero in 45 BC. Printers have used it as filler since the
1500s, and it spread to digital design through desktop publishing software that
shipped it as sample content.

## Why it is nonsense on purpose

Because the Latin is garbled, it reads as texture rather than language. Real
copy pulls a reviewer into editing sentences; placeholder copy keeps the
conversation on the design. Its letter distribution and word lengths also stay
close enough to English that a block of it looks like a realistic paragraph.

## A sample

> Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, consectetur adipiscing elit, sed do eiusmod tempor
> incididunt ut labore et dolore magna aliqua. Ut enim ad minim veniam, quis
> nostrud exercitation ullamco laboris nisi ut aliquip ex ea commodo consequat.
> Duis aute irure dolor in reprehenderit in voluptate velit esse cillum dolore eu
> fugiat nulla pariatur.

## Using it well

- Match the length of the placeholder to the length you expect the real text to
  be, so the layout does not shift later.
- Swap in real content before any usability test, since nonsense text hides
  problems with reading and comprehension.
- Never ship it. Stray lorem ipsum in production is a common and very visible
  bug.
